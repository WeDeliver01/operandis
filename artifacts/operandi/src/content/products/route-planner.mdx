## What it is

Route Planner is the morning-planning tool for a working courier business. Every day, a dispatcher faces the same puzzle: dozens of pickups and deliveries, a handful of drivers, customers who only want to be visited at certain times, and a fleet with limited capacity. Who goes where, in what order, in which vehicle?

For most couriers, the answer is a whiteboard, a printed list, and a WhatsApp group. Route Planner replaces all of that with software that does the puzzle automatically — and gets better at it every day.

## The shape we built

A dispatcher opens Route Planner first thing in the morning, drops in the day's stops, and within seconds gets a full plan: every driver, every route, in the right order, on a map. The system respects the rules of the real business — driver shift lengths, vehicle capacity, customer time windows, depot return rules — so the plan it produces is actually drivable, not just theoretically optimal.

When the day starts to deviate from the plan — a stop gets cancelled, a new one comes in, a driver runs late — the dispatcher doesn't have to rebuild anything. They drag the change in, and the routes recalculate around it instantly.

Each route can be sent straight to the driver's phone with one click, so the handoff from the dispatcher to the driver is seamless. No printed sheets, no copy-and-paste into WhatsApp.

We built it as an internal tool first, but with a clean separation between the rules of *this particular* business and the optimisation brain underneath — so the same engine could one day power a stand-alone product for other couriers.
