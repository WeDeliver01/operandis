## What it is

Delectables is an online marketplace for Gauteng's independent bakers. Customers browse verified bakeries across Johannesburg, Pretoria, Midrand, Kempton Park and beyond, order celebration cakes, everyday treats, artisan breads and custom bakes, and have them delivered to their door.

Many of the best bakers in the province work from home kitchens and small shopfronts. They have the skill but not the storefront: orders arrive through WhatsApp, prices get worked out by hand, and delivery is improvised. Delectables gives each of them a proper shop window and checkout, and gives customers one place to find them.

## The shape we built

There are two sides to the product. Customers search and browse by category, see each bakery's location, opening hours and rating, and check out from a single basket. Bakers apply through a vendor onboarding flow and run their shop from a dashboard: menu, products and incoming orders.

Custom cakes are the hardest thing to sell online, so they get their own tool. Customers choose size, flavour, filling and finish, and the price updates live as they build, so there is no back-and-forth to get a quote.

Delivery runs on a daily schedule. A countdown on the home page shows how long is left to order for tomorrow's run, and customers can track their order once it's on its way.
